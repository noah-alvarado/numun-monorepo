Northwestern University Model United Nations (NUMUN) is an entirely student-run
organization. We host an annual conference for high school delegates and field a
competitive collegiate travel team that represents Northwestern at conferences
across the country.

## What we do

- **Host a conference.** Each spring, NUMUN welcomes delegations from across the
  country for a weekend of general assemblies, specialized agencies, and crisis
  committees.
- **Compete nationally.** Our travel team sends delegates to top collegiate MUN
  conferences throughout the year.
- **Build community.** From novice committees to seasoned crisis arcs, we aim to
  make MUN approachable for first-timers and challenging for veterans.

## Our commitment

NUMUN is committed to running an inclusive, equitable conference. We offer
financial aid for qualifying delegations and continually work to lower the
barriers to participating in Model United Nations.
