Northwestern's collegiate travel team represents the University at Model United
Nations conferences across the country. Our delegates compete in committees
ranging from large general assemblies to specialized crisis cabinets, bringing
back awards and experience that strengthen the whole club.

We welcome delegates of all experience levels. If you're a Northwestern student
interested in joining, reach out at outreach@numun.org.
