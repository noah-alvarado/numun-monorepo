NUMUN brings together hundreds of delegates each spring for a weekend of
substantive debate. Our committees range from large general assemblies to
specialized agencies and fast-paced crisis rooms, with novice committees
designed for first-time delegates.

Registration is handled by faculty advisers on behalf of their school. Browse the
[committees](/committees) on offer, then register your delegation to secure your
spot.
