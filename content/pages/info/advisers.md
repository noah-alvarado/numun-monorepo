title: For Advisers
intro: Everything you need to bring a delegation to NUMUN.
sections:
  - heading: Registering your delegation
    body: >-
      Delegations are registered by a faculty adviser through the NUMUN portal.
      You'll provide your school, an estimated delegate count (including any
      financially qualifying delegates), and delegation-level committee
      preferences. NUMUN staff review and approve each registration before you
      finalize your delegate roster.
  - heading: Finalizing your roster
    body: >-
      Once approved, advisers add delegates in bulk via CSV, XLSX, or a Google
      Sheets link, then confirm the parsed roster in the portal. Committee and
      country assignments are proposed by staff based on your delegation's
      preferences and experience.
  - heading: Financial policy
    body: >-
      Conference fees are billed per delegate. Financial aid is available for
      qualifying delegations — indicate your financially qualifying count during
      registration. Payment is handled off-platform; the portal tracks your
      balance and paid status.
