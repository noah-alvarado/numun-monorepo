NUMUN is Northwestern University's Model United Nations organization. Each spring
we host hundreds of high school delegates from across the country for a weekend of
substantive debate across general assemblies, specialized agencies, and fast-paced
crisis committees.

We also send a competitive collegiate travel team to conferences nationwide, where
Northwestern students compete against the best programs in the country. Whether
you're an adviser, a delegate, or a prospective staffer, there's a place for you
at NUMUN.
